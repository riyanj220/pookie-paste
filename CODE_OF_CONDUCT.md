# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Pookie Paste community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for people participating in the project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Helping new contributors participate effectively

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or intimidation
- Personal attacks
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

Project spaces include:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Repository comments
- Community communication channels

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported privately to the project maintainers.

Do not use public issues to report sensitive conduct matters.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.

Maintainers are expected to respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may consider factors including:

- Severity of the behavior
- Whether the behavior was intentional
- Whether similar behavior has occurred previously
- Whether the individual accepts responsibility
- Potential impact on contributors and the community

Possible responses include:

- Private correction
- Formal warning
- Temporary restriction
- Permanent removal from project spaces
